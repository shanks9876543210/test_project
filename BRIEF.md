# Build task

**Time:** 2 hours. **AI tools:** allowed and expected. Use whatever you normally use.

This repository is the starting point of a billing service that a few hundred shops will start
using next month. From today you own it. Your job is to add the two features below and leave the
codebase in a state you'd be comfortable putting in front of real customers.

## Feature 1: Returns

Shop owners keep asking how to handle a customer who brings goods back. Some return everything,
some return one item out of five. Some have already paid, some haven't. Owners need the books,
the stock and the customer's balance to be right afterwards, and their accountant needs a
document for each return.

## Feature 2: Payment reminders

Owners spend hours every week calling customers about unpaid invoices. They want the system to
remind customers by SMS when an invoice is overdue. Customers who get several messages a day, or
messages at night, complain to the shop, and the shop complains to us.

## What we expect

- Working code on top of this repository. Keep the existing API working.
- Tests for the parts you think matter most.
- A short `NOTES.md` with:
  - the questions you would have asked us and the assumptions you made instead,
  - anything you changed in the existing code, and why,
  - what you'd do next with another day.

The descriptions above are deliberately short, like real requests from the business. Where
something is unclear, you can ask us, or decide and write your decision down. Both are fine.

You will walk us through your work for 30 minutes afterwards. Be ready to explain any line you
submit, including lines your AI tools wrote.
